Clock Skew and Jitter.
clock skew: spatial variation. different synced clock signals are out of skew, out of sync.
it is the difference between clocks.

clock jitter: the variation for one clock signal output - might be sped up or slowed down but in comparison to itself.

skew = spatial effect, two flipflops
jitter - temporal effect, one flip flop.

positive skew: positive edge from 1 arrives before receiving edge at 2. (if flipflop1 before flipflop2)
change at edge 1 on graph - sampled at edge 4.
clock time larger, more relaxed.
