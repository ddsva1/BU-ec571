Energy consumption in CMOS circuit: example.
to calculate energy from the power source:
integrate from 0 to t time (Vdd*Idd)(time t) dt

= Integrate from 0 to Vdd(Vdd Capacitor L (dVout/dt)) dt
= Cl*Vdd^2

Energy stored in the capacitor:
E = Integrate from 0 to t time (Vout C * d Vout/d t) d time
= Integrate from 0 to Vdd (Vout C) d Vout
= 1/2 C*Vdd^2

if capacitor charged and discharged 10 times, total amount of energy pulled from power supply is 10 C Vdd^2.
When discharging, no power pulled from power supply.
